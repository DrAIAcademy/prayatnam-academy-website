Our Key Difference

AI-Integrated Learning That Supports Regular Subjects

Artificial Intelligence is not used as a shortcut. It is introduced as a learning assistant that helps students understand concepts, ask better questions, revise efficiently and connect classroom topics with real-world technology.

  • Students discover how Mathematics supports data science, algorithms and machine learning.
  • Science concepts are connected with robotics, automation, simulation and intelligent systems.
  • English is strengthened through communication, research, structured writing and responsible prompt creation.
  • Social Science and Humanities are linked with research, ethics, society and the impact of technology.
  • Students learn responsible and age-appropriate use of AI tools for projects, revision and doubt clarification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Parents Commonly Ask

Which classes are taught at Prayatnam Academy?

We provide regular coaching for students from Class 1 to Class 12.

Which subjects are included for Classes 1 to 8?

All major school subjects are covered for Classes 1 to 8. The exact subject combination can be confirmed according to the student's school curriculum.

Which subjects are available for Classes 9 and 10?

We provide coaching in Mathematics, Science, Social Science and English for Classes 9 and 10.

Which subjects are available for Classes 11 and 12?

Subject-wise coaching is available for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Humanities subjects.

Is Artificial Intelligence included with regular studies?

Age-appropriate AI applications are aligned with regular subjects to help students understand concepts and use modern learning tools responsibly.

Are separate AI classes available?

Yes. Separate AI batches are available for students from Class 6 to Class 12.

What are the monthly fees?

Fees are ₹1,000 per month for Classes 1–5, ₹1,500 for Classes 6–8, ₹2,000 for Classes 9–10, and ₹1,200 per subject for Classes 11–12.

Where is Prayatnam Academy located?

Prayatnam Academy is located in Raj Nagar 2, Palam, Street No. 12, near Kennedy Public School, New Delhi.
